# User manual

Sign in with the account provided by the property administrator. Use the Lao/EN switch in the header at any time; the preference is remembered on the device.

Start by adding buildings and rooms. Add a tenant, create a draft contract for an available room, and activate it through check-in. The database prevents two current contracts for one room.

For monthly work, record each room's water and electricity readings, then generate its invoice. To receive money, open Payments, select an outstanding invoice, enter the amount and method, and confirm. Partial payments keep the invoice open with the correct remaining balance. Print the receipt after saving.

Record deposits in the deposit ledger, not as rental revenue. During check-out, enter final meters, outstanding charges, inspection damage, and other deductions. Review the calculated refund before confirming.

Use Maintenance for repair tickets and Expenses for operating costs. Reports can be filtered and exported to CSV. Contact an administrator when an action is hidden or denied; access depends on your assigned role.
